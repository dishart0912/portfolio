Overview

About the Project

Fokus is a hydration drink brand by Nishchay Malhan and Abhishek Malhan. This project is a personal UI/UX exploration where I recreated and reimagined their product landing page in Figma.

The goal was to experiment with visual hierarchy, colour-driven interaction, and modern landing page design — transforming a simple product showcase into a more engaging and aesthetically refined digital experience.

This was not a brand redesign, but a design interpretation focused on interface and interaction design.
