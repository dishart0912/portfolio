Payment Flow UI

A clean, dark-mode fintech experience designed to make payments feel effortless and trustworthy — built across two animation approaches, exploring swipeable card carousels and list-based selection flows.

Overview

About the Project

Designing payment flows requires a precise balance — users need to feel secure without feeling slowed down. This project explored that tension across a dark-mode mobile interface, where clarity, trust signals, and interaction fluidity were the primary design pillars.

The project was developed in two distinct animation approaches — each exploring a different mental model for how users select and confirm their payment card before checkout.

Fintech Mobile UI Dark Mode UX Design Figma Interaction Design

Problem

Problem Statement

Payment screens are where users are most anxious. Cluttered layouts, unclear card selection states, and weak trust signals all drive abandonment at the most critical moment in the user journey.

The challenge: design a multi-card wallet interface that lets users select, switch, and confirm payments with confidence — while keeping the UI clean, focused, and fast to navigate.

An additional constraint was making the card selection interaction feel physically intuitive — not just functional. The UI needed to communicate which card was active and what the next action was, without any ambiguity.

Solution

Design Solution

The solution was built in two interaction approaches — both sharing the same dark-navy visual language, card-forward layout, and trust indicators, but differing in how the user navigates their wallet.

The key principle: the card is the hero — it takes center stage with clear balance display, card type branding, and immediate access to the Proceed action.

Interaction Model

Tap to Select

Cards are shown in a stacked fan view. Tapping selects the card and reveals a Proceed button. Tapping Proceed navigates to a dedicated Payment Details screen with full order summary before the final pay action.

Design Rationale

Explicit confirmation

The two-step flow (select → confirm) gives users a clear moment to review before committing. The order summary on the final screen builds confidence with a security badge and encrypted payment indicator.

A carousel swipe model — cards are stacked vertically and the user swipes right on their favourite to bring it to focus. The active card expands to show full details with a direct Pay action.

Interaction Model

Swipe to Select

Cards live in a vertically stacked list. Swiping right on any card brings it forward — it expands to reveal full balance, card number, and expiry. The active state makes the selection crystal clear.

Design Rationale

Gesture-forward

The swipe gesture maps naturally to the real-world action of pulling out a card. It gives each card a moment in the spotlight — reducing cognitive load by showing only one card's full details at a time.

Process

Design Process

The process started by mapping every anxiety point in a payment flow — from "which card is active?" to "did my payment go through?". Competitive analysis of fintech apps like PhonePe, Razorpay, and Apple Pay informed the design decisions.

Two distinct interaction paradigms were prototyped in Figma — the swipe model and the tap-select model. Both were tested against the same user goal: selecting a card and completing payment in under 5 seconds.

A multi-step payment wizard that feels effortless — with clear security signals, smart error handling, and a confirmation experience that delights.

Design Decisions

Key Design Decisions

✔ Dark Navy Base: Communicates trust, premium quality, and reduces eye strain in a high-focus task environment.

✔ Coloured Card Gradients: Each card has a unique gradient (green Amex, red Mastercard, purple Visa) — making visual identification instant without reading the card number.

✔ Balance Displayed Prominently: Users need context to choose the right card. Showing the balance on the card face eliminates the need to remember or navigate away.

✔ Green Proceed CTA: Green is universally associated with "go" — the CTA colour was a deliberate trust and action signal, standing out clearly against the dark background.

✔ Security Badge: The "Secure Payment" indicator and "Your payment information is securely encrypted" footer text directly address the user's primary anxiety at checkout.

✔ Add New Card Flow: Included in Approach 1 — the add-card screen follows minimal form design with clear field labels, reducing friction for new card entry.
