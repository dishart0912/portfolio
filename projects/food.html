Process

Design Process

Studied top food apps to identify what works and what frustrates users. Prioritized the discovery flow — helping users find food they'll love before they even know what they want. Checkout was reduced to just two taps from cart.

An intuitive food discovery and ordering experience with a visually rich design language that makes every dish look irresistible.
