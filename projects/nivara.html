Nivara

Nivara is a seller-centric platform designed to empower small businesses by streamlining operations through an intuitive, timeline-based interface. This project leverages strategic UI/UX design to bridge the gap between local entrepreneurs and digital growth, fostering both high engagement and operational efficiency.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Timeline
4 Weeks
Tools
Figma
Platform
Web

Problem

Problem Statement

Small businesses often struggle to sell online due to lack of trust, visibility, and complex onboarding systems. Existing e-commerce platforms allow instant product listings, which increases the risk of fake or low-quality products, ultimately reducing user trust.

There is a need for a platform that not only empowers sellers but also ensures a safe and reliable shopping experience for users.

Solution

Design Solution

Nivara introduces a moderated marketplace model where product listings are verified before going live. This ensures higher trust, reduces fraudulent listings, and maintains platform quality.

The platform is divided into three core interfaces — User, Seller, and Admin — each designed to simplify workflows while maintaining control and transparency across the system.

The key idea was to balance ease of selling with platform trust.

Overview

About the Project

Nivara is a moderated e-commerce platform designed to empower small businesses while maintaining platform trust. Sellers can list products, but every listing goes through an admin approval system before going live. This ensures quality control, prevents fake or misleading products, and creates a reliable shopping experience for users.

Figma E-commerce UI Design Product Design

System Flow

How the Platform Works

1. Seller Listing: Sellers add product details, images, pricing, and inventory.

2. Admin Moderation: Instead of going live instantly, the product enters a verification queue where the admin reviews it for quality, accuracy, and authenticity.

3. Approval & Publishing: Once approved, the product becomes visible to users in the marketplace.

4. Continuous Control: The admin retains the ability to remove or disable products at any time, ensuring ongoing platform integrity and preventing misuse.

5. User Experience: Users can confidently browse and purchase products, knowing that all listings are verified.

Features

Key Features

✔ Admin Approval System: Ensures only verified products are visible to users.

✔ Seller Dashboard: Allows sellers to manage products, orders, and business details efficiently.

✔ Secure Shopping Experience: Users can browse and purchase with confidence due to moderated listings.

✔ Continuous Control: Admin can remove or disable products anytime to maintain platform quality.

✔ Clean & Minimal UI: Designed to reduce cognitive load and improve usability across all user types.

Process

Design Process

The design process began by identifying a key gap in existing e-commerce platforms — the lack of trust caused by unverified product listings. I focused on creating a system that balances seller freedom with platform control.

I first mapped out the complete flow between three user roles — User, Seller, and Admin — to understand how data and actions move across the system. This helped in structuring the platform into three interconnected panels.

Low-fidelity wireframes were created to define layout and navigation, followed by high-fidelity UI design in Figma. Special attention was given to simplifying the checkout process and making seller actions intuitive.

A major focus was designing a system that feels simple for users while handling complex logic in the background.
