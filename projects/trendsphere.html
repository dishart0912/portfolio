Overview

About the Project

TrendSphere is a mobile news application designed during my internship, built end-to-end — from initial wireframes in Miro to polished high-fidelity screens in Figma. The goal was to create a reading experience that feels effortless: curated feeds, clean typography, and navigation that gets out of the way.

The app targets modern readers who want relevant news without clutter — an editorial product with strong visual hierarchy and a personalized browsing experience across 10 key screens.

Problem

Problem Statement

News apps today are overwhelmingly noisy — cluttered feeds, clickbait headlines, no real personalization, and reading experiences optimized for ad impressions rather than comprehension.

Additionally, the given problem statement emphasized the need for structured organization — requiring news content to be saved and managed in folder-wise categories for better accessibility and user control.

There was a clear gap for a reader-first news app: one that prioritizes clean reading, smart content filtering, seamless navigation, and intuitive content organization over advertising or engagement manipulation.

Solution

Design Solution

TrendSphere simplifies the news consumption experience through strong information hierarchy, category-based filtering, and a bookmark system for readers who want to save and revisit articles. The design focuses on low-friction navigation — every key destination is reachable in one tap from the bottom navigation bar.

The key design decision: treat every screen as an editorial surface — where typography, spacing, and imagery do the work, not decoration.

Process

Design Process

The design process began with user interviews and competitive analysis of existing news apps. Low-fidelity wireframes were sketched to map out information architecture and user flows before any visual design decisions were made.

The wireframe stage allowed rapid iteration on layouts — especially for the homepage hierarchy (breaking news vs. trending vs. category feeds) and the article reading experience. Once the structure was locked, high-fidelity screens were built in Figma with a strong focus on typographic hierarchy and reading comfort.

A clean reading experience with smart content grouping and seamless category-based browsing — designed to keep readers in flow.

Features

Key Design Decisions

✔ Animated Splash Screen: Creates a polished first impression and masks initial loading — keeping the brand moment intentional.

✔ Seamless Login / Sign Up: Minimal friction onboarding — single-screen toggle between login and signup states without page navigation.

✔ Homepage Feed: Content hierarchy separates breaking news, trending, and category sections with clear visual differentiation.

✔ Search Page: Real-time search with category filters and trending topics surfaced as suggestions.

✔ Bookmarks: One-tap save from any article; a dedicated page for saved reading with offline-first intent.

✔ Filter Page: Topic and source preferences managed in a single sheet — so the home feed feels personally curated.

✔ Account Page: Reading stats, preferences, and notification settings in a clean settings-style layout.

✔ Article Detail: Distraction-free reading mode with estimated read time, share, and bookmark actions within reach.
